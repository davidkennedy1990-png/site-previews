The workshop

Looking after the Coast since 2003.

Rod Harman Motors is a small, local workshop in Stanmore Bay, Silverdale. You talk to the people working on your car, get a straight answer, and drive away knowing what was done.

Locals bring us everything from the family hatchback and the 4WD to the caravan before summer and the classic in the garage.

Services
