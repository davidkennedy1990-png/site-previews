Welcome to Millies

Cafe and restaurant, together.

Millies cares about the whole community. We serve the best quality coffee, food and drinks in a stress-free, welcoming space for you to meet, eat and drink.

The space is modern and spacious with a relaxed feel and an elegant setting, and our warm, friendly team look after you from your first flat white to your last glass of wine.

  • All-day breakfast and lunch menu, plus a full cabinet
  • Indoor and outdoor tables on Millwater Parkway
  • Kids' menu and the Millies loyalty app
