Meet your physio

Hi, I'm Nicol.

I've worked as a women's health physiotherapist since 1997, including fifteen years as a Senior Pelvic Health Physiotherapist for National Women's Health at Auckland City Hospital. Today I see people privately in Silverdale and Remuera.

Pelvic issues are common, but many people put up with them in silence. My goal is to help you understand what's going on and give you the tools to manage your own pelvic health with confidence.

  • Qualified in physiotherapy at the University of Northumbria, UK (1995)
  • Western Acupuncture, AUT (2003)
  • Teaches "Physiotherapy for Incontinence" at Auckland Medical School
  • Member of the New Zealand Physiotherapy Society and its Pelvic, Women's & Men's Health special interest group
What I treat

Care for every chapter.

Treatment plans, practical advice and collaboration with your doctor or specialist, tailored to you.

Pregnancy & postnatal

Pre- and post-natal assessment, perineal tears and scar pain, and safe screening for your return to exercise.

Bladder & bowel

Incontinence, urgency and frequency, with clear, practical steps you can keep up at home.

Pelvic pain

Endometriosis, painful bladder syndrome, menstrual-related and post-surgical pain, and pudendal neuralgia.

Prolapse support

Pelvic floor strengthening, lifestyle advice and a pessary fitting service for vaginal heaviness or prolapse.

Sexual health

Sensitive, confidential care for painful intercourse, vaginismus and vulvodynia.

Men's health

Pre- and post-prostatectomy rehab, bladder and bowel function, prostatitis and pelvic pain.

Acupuncture is also available for chronic pelvic pain, overactive and painful bladder, prostatitis, vaginismus and vulvodynia.

Keep moving

Exercise with confidence, at any age.

Risk assessment and practical advice to help prevent incontinence and prolapse while you stay active.

Your first visit

What to expect.

Many people feel a little nervous before their first pelvic health appointment. That's completely normal. You'll always be in control of what happens.

How long is an appointment?

Initial appointments are usually an hour. Follow-ups can be shorter, 30 or 45 minutes.

Do I need a referral?

No, you can refer yourself. Nicol will keep in touch with your GP and let you know if she thinks you should see them.

Will I need an internal examination?

It's often the best way to understand what will help, but it's never required. Nicol will always offer you alternatives.

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes, you're always welcome to bring a support person. Children are welcome too.

Is it covered by ACC or insurance?

Some childbirth injuries and treatment injuries can be covered by ACC, and Nicol can help with the paperwork. Southern Cross can be invoiced directly; for other insurers you'll pay on the day and claim it back.

What should I wear?

Loose, comfortable clothing (not jeans). Shorts are handy if you have them.
