About Kaid

Local, detailed and process-driven

Kaid specialises in residential sales across the Hibiscus Coast – a place he’s proudly called home and been an active part of for over a decade. Backed by a career sales volume exceeding $150 million and numerous industry awards, he has a reliable and strong track record.

With previous experience in both residential and commercial development land sales, he has assisted a wide variety of clients with many types of negotiations. Kaid provides trusted advice and consistently puts clients’ needs first to secure favourable outcomes.

Kaid is highly process-driven, with a disciplined approach. No stone is left unturned, ensuring homes are positioned correctly, marketed effectively, and negotiated with precision.
